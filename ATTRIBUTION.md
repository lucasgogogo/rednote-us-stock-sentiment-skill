For original project material from v2.0.0, preserve the following reasonable author-attribution notice in that material or in the Appropriate Legal Notices displayed by works containing it, as permitted by AGPL-3.0 section 7(b):

> Rednote US-stock sentiment skill — Copyright (C) 2026 lucasgogogo.
> Original project / 原项目：https://github.com/lucasgogogo/rednote-us-stock-sentiment-skill

分发修改版时，同时清楚标出修改及相关日期，保留适用的许可证、版权和上游署名；对应源码提供义务见LICENSE第4–6及13条。README、NOTICE或“关于/许可证”法律通知是可读的署名位置，不要求另行购买推广或宣称获得原作者背书。

For modified distributions, also state the changes and relevant dates, retain applicable license/copyright/upstream notices, and meet the Corresponding Source requirements in LICENSE sections 4–6 and 13. README, NOTICE or an About/Licenses legal-notice location may carry readable attribution. No paid promotion or claim of endorsement is required.

此要求仅适用于本项目作者有权授权的原创材料；第三方代码/字体/标志保留各自许可。本条不追溯改变v1.0.0的Apache许可，也不把“每张报告展示推广logo”追加成AGPL法律义务。

This applies only to original material the project author can license. Third-party components retain their own terms. It does not retroactively change v1.0.0 or impose an advertising-logo requirement on every report as a legal condition.
