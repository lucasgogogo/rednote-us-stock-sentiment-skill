“公开源码”在这里指按许可向接收者或相应网络用户提供对应源码，并给予其继续传播的权利；不一定必须上传到GitHub，也不要求无条件向全世界发布纯私人改动。独立、单独聚合且不构成受许可覆盖作品的程序，不会仅因放在同一目录就全部变成AGPL。

Here, source disclosure means providing Corresponding Source to recipients or relevant remote users under the license, with redistribution rights. It does not always require a GitHub upload or worldwide publication of private changes. Separate independent aggregates do not become covered merely by sharing a directory.

对应源码包括生成、安装、运行和修改程序所需的相关源码和脚本，不是用户的登录凭据、个人账户账单或采集得到的私有raw。运行程序不会仅因使用工具就自动改变输入数据的许可；输出是否构成受许可覆盖作品取决于其具体内容，按LICENSE第2条处理。

Corresponding Source includes the relevant program/build/install/run/modify source and scripts, not user login credentials, private bills or scraped raw data. Running the tool alone does not relicense input data; whether an output is a covered work depends on its content under section 2.
